## Description
**en**
UTUVO Panel is a single Home Screen widget in the new iOS 27 extra-large portrait size.

WHAT'S ON IT
• Time and date (optionally with seconds)
• Your next calendar event
• Local weather with high/low
• Steps, exercise and stand rings
• A timer with real Play / Pause / Stop buttons
• Five app shortcuts that open their apps directly
• A system row you compose from CPU, memory, storage, network, battery, Low Power Mode and thermal state

LOOKS LIKE PART OF iOS
Every tile and button is Liquid Glass artwork. With the Clear icon style the whole panel turns to glass; on a full-colour Home Screen, pick your wallpaper once and the panel blends in.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no analytics, no tracking. Weather uses a coarse location (about 1 km) sent to Open-Meteo. Calendar, location and health data never leave your device.

Requires iOS 27.

**zh-Hant**
UTUVO Panel 是一塊放在桌面上的小工具，用 iOS 27 全新的特大直式尺寸。

面板上有
• 時間與日期（可走秒）
• 下一筆行事曆行程
• 所在地天氣與最高／最低溫
• 步數、運動、站立三環
• 真的能按的計時器（開始／暫停／停止）
• 五個常用 app，點了直接開
• 系統列：CPU、記憶體、儲存空間、連線、電量、低耗電、溫度，任選四項

像 iOS 自己的東西
每一顆磚和按鈕都是 Liquid Glass。圖示樣式選「透明」時整塊面板變玻璃；全彩桌面則選一次桌布，面板就融進去。

不收資料
沒有帳號、沒有分析、不追蹤。天氣只把約 1 公里精度的位置送給 Open-Meteo；行事曆、定位、健康資料都留在裝置上。

需要 iOS 27。

## Review notes（給審核員）
```
UTUVO Panel is a WidgetKit widget in the iOS 27 systemExtraLargePortrait family.
To see it: add the widget from the widget gallery (search "UTUVO Panel"), choose the tallest size.
App shortcuts open Apple's apps via their public URL schemes (music://, maps://, ichat://, calshow://, weather://, fitnessapp://…) using OpenURLIntent.
The "transparent" background is the user's own wallpaper photo cropped by the app; no screenshot of other apps is taken.
Weather: Open-Meteo, coarse coordinates (2 decimals). No account, no analytics.
```

## 審核風險（先講）
1. **5.2.5 模仿 Apple app icon**：五磚（Music／Messages／Maps／Camera／Notes）是自繪但長得像。被退就把那五顆換成抽象符號版。
2. URL scheme 開 Apple app：公開 scheme，一般會過；`App-prefs://` 已移除。
3. HealthKit：只讀、有用途說明；Health 資料只顯示不上傳。
